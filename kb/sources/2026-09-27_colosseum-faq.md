# Colosseum's FAQ: videos, repository access, prior work

## Key facts

- **Pitch video:** "A two-to-three-minute presentation video. This is one of the first resources
  judges review".
- **Demo video:** "A product-demo video of no more than three minutes explaining how the product
  works".
- **Repository:** "Open-source repositories are encouraged, but private repositories are allowed if
  access is granted to hackathon@colosseum.com for review."
- **Prior work:** "Builders may use pre-existing code, but teams must disclose all relevant past
  development work in the submission form."
- **What judges weigh:** Founder + Market Fit, Insight, Product + Execution, Potential Market Size,
  Founder Communication, Viability, Traction.

## What it supports

- `docs/submission/SUBMISSION.md`: the disclosure, the video lengths, and the repository-access
  choice.
- `docs/submission/PITCH.md` and `docs/submission/DEMO.md`: their length limits.

## Caveats

- `docs/HACKATHON.md` earlier cited a Colosseum blog post for video lengths. This FAQ is newer and
  specific, so it wins where they differ.
