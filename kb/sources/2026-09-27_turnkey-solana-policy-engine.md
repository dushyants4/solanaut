# Turnkey's Solana policy engine

## Key facts

- The engine parses "All Accounts included / All programs invoked / All SOL Transfers in the
  Transaction, with Sender, Recipient Amounts / All instruction call data within the transaction".
- The blog post says nothing about SPL tokens, time windows or lookup tables.

## Caveats

- A research agent also read Turnkey's agentic-wallet docs (undated), which show per-transaction caps,
  destination limits and human co-approval, with nothing allowed unless a policy allows it, and no
  time-window totals. That part is **via-agent**.
