# SEAL Safe Harbor and SEAL 911

The Security Alliance's frameworks for whitehat rescues and emergency help, named in the readiness plan.

## Key facts

- Safe Harbor gives legal protection to whitehats who recover assets during an active exploit, when there is no time for disclosure.
- Rescued funds go to the protocol's asset recovery address.
- SEAL 911 is an emergency line for urgent security help and for disclosing critical vulnerabilities.
