# Crypto World's Fair: the page

## Key facts

- **Deadline:** submissions are due 2026-10-12.
- **Prizes:** Grand Prize $30,000; the next 20 projects $15,000 each; Public Good Prize $5,000;
  University Prize $5,000. "Total prize pool: $840,000".
- **Solana track:** "$100,000 pool (10 projects × $10,000 each)".
- **Other ecosystem tracks:** Ethereum, Hyperliquid, Base, Tempo, Arbitrum, Zcash and Robinhood
  Chain. The fair is multi-chain.
- **Accelerator:** "All Hackathon winners will be interviewed and considered for Colosseum's
  accelerator program"; accepted teams get "$250,000 pre-seed funding" and 12 weeks in San Francisco.
