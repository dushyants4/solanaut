## 5. What failed or surprised

- **The benign battery found a defect in both sensors.** Its first run stopped 94.5% of benign payments: a recurring
  payment read as a retry. One definition changed, no coefficient, and the battery was re-run on fresh seeds.
- **The latch was derived, not recorded,** so a guard restored under a more lenient policy came back unlatched. The
  tripping decision now records `latch: true`.
- **D13:** a send of unknown fate was reported `failed`, which frees a payment that may still land. Closed.
- **D14, found by devnet:** Solana Agent Kit reports landed payments as failed (M13). Behind a sign-only integration,
  the guard would then have admitted the agent's retry once the first attempt expired. Closed test-first.
- **The kit signs each plugin payment twice** (M12). The guard refuses the second signature, rightly.
- **Today's latch rule stops the demo.** One early re-sign latches the guard on every devnet run, until the operator
  signs (ADR-012).
- **Not stopped by Keel:** a loop alternating two shapes with a changing amount. The window caps and the INHIBIT
  clamp bound it.
- **The public devnet RPC rate-limits per method.** It stopped the first drill. Reads now back off; sends never retry.
- **The devnet faucet refused this machine,** so 2a.9 and 2a.10 waited for the human to fund the operator.
- **Tooling:**
  - A 3-agent workflow for the independent run failed on the account's usage limit within seconds.
  - Outside agent CLIs (codex, agy, grok, pi, hermes) were refused by the auto-mode classifier as unsafe to run
    unattended, even jailed.
  - The independent run went to this session's subagents instead (section 3).
- **Small:**
  - The boundary guard's Bash heuristic refused about five legitimate commands. It failed closed, as designed.
  - A local validator's slow status read inflated a revoke time fourfold; slots are now recorded beside it.
  - The ADR-012 draft first misnamed `v` as a rule version.
- **One mutant no test here can catch:** `fsync`, whose effect shows only on power loss. S6 says "a crash", never
  "a power cut".
